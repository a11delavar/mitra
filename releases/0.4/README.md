This release adds a year view, connects Mitra to the calendars most people already have, Google, Apple and Notion, brings people into your events, and makes Mitra at home on a phone.

## Year view
The whole year on one screen, every day a small cell, for finding the free week or seeing a season at once.

Docs: [Year view](../../docs/views/year.md)

## Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Notion
Sign in with Google, add an app-specific password for iCloud, or paste a Notion token, and your calendars and databases sit side by side in one week. A Notion view becomes a calendar, and a page's body becomes its entry's description.

## Time zones per entry
An entry can keep its own time zone, like a flight that leaves in one and lands in another. The extra time zones beside the week tuck away, and swipe back when you want them.
