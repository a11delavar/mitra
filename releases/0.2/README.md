This release brings entries that repeat, and lets an entry move from one calendar to another.

## Repeating entries
An entry can repeat every day, week, month or year, or on a rule of your own: every second week on Monday and Thursday, or the last Friday of each month. It can stop after a number of times or on a date, and a single occurrence can be skipped. Change one occurrence, and Mitra asks whether you mean just this one, this one and the ones after it, or all of them. The rules travel with your CalDAV calendar both ways, so the calendars you already use repeat the same.
