Mitra 0.9 is still being planned. It brings bigger pieces of work together and shows what can be done next, lets the sidebar follow the way you think, and writes dates the way you read them.

## Better subtasks
An entry's subtasks share a lane in the month and year views, so a project reads as one line, with its short subtasks folded into it. The table nests subtasks under their parent, and the year view can hide them. A task's progress weighs each subtask by its size, so a long piece of work counts for more than a quick one, and an event shows the progress of the tasks under it. A subtask without a due date of its own takes its parent's.

## Custom calendar grouping
Group the calendars in the sidebar your own way, beside grouping them by account, and hide or show a whole group at once.

## Advanced locale builder
Choose more than a language and region: the calendar your dates are written in, the digits, the first day of the week and the clock. A second calendar, such as the Persian one, can show beside your dates, and you can set your current time zone by hand.

## Tasks that can be done now
Planning lists the open tasks no other open task blocks in a section of their own, so it's clear what you can work on right now.

## Warning on blocked tasks
Completing a task that another open task still blocks asks first, so finishing out of order is a choice and not an accident.

## TickTick integration
Connect your TickTick account, and its lists come into Mitra as task calendars, kept in sync both ways.

## Notifications where you are
Reminders reach the tools you already use, such as ntfy, Apprise or your own webhook.

## More languages
Mitra is now available in Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese.
