Mitra 0.9 shows how bigger pieces of work come together.

## Better subtasks
An entry's subtasks share a lane in the month and year views, so a project reads as one line, with its short subtasks folded into it. The table nests subtasks under their parent, and the year view can hide them. A task's progress weighs each subtask by its size, so a long piece of work counts for more than a quick one. A subtask without a due date of its own takes its parent's.
