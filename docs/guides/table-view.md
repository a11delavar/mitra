---
title: Table view
description: Your entries as rows — pick the days to list, sort and filter them, and change many at once.
---

The **Table** view lists your entries as rows instead of drawing them on a grid. Open it with <kbd>S</kbd> or from the view picker.

Every row is one entry — each occurrence of a repeating entry is a row of its own — and its title is the same chip the calendar draws: click it to open the entry's editor.

## Which entries

The table lists the entries of a span of days counted from today, named in the page heading: **Past 30 days**, **Today**, **Next 7 days**, **Next 30 days** (where it starts), **Next 12 months**, **All entries**, or a **Custom range** of two dates. Choose it in the **When** column's menu. The count beside the search field is how many rows are listed.

Tasks without a date, and open tasks that are overdue, are listed whichever span you choose, so the default shows the backlog and the coming month together.

**All entries** is for tidying up. A repeating entry is one row there, standing for the whole series: its **When** says where it starts, and moving or deleting it moves or deletes every occurrence. Its status stays with each occurrence, so the status buttons skip it.

**Today** in the page header, and <kbd>G</kbd> for another date, scroll to that day's first row without changing the span.

## Search, sort and filter

The **search** field matches every word you type against the title, the location, the description and the calendar.

Click a column's heading for its menu:

- **Sort ascending** or **Sort descending** — choose the one in force again to remove it. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to sort by several columns.
- The column's **filter**, where it has one: untick the values to leave out. Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> to keep just the one you click. The **Status** filter's **No status** stands for events, so unticking it lists tasks alone.
- **Hide column**, which also lifts the column's filter.

The **Status**, **Calendar**, **Type** and **Repeats** columns can filter, and **When** holds the span of days. A funnel beside a heading shows the column is leaving rows out. **Title** and **When** cannot be hidden.

## Columns

The table starts with **Title**, **When**, **Calendar**, **Status**, **Location**, **Participants**, **Subtask of** and **Blocked by**. The button at the end of the heading row shows the others — **Duration**, **Type**, **Repeats**, **Reminders** and **Description** — and **Reset columns** puts the defaults back.

Every column is as wide as its content. Drag a heading to move its column, and its edge to resize it; double-click the edge to fit the column to its content again.

## Change many at once

Click a row to select it, hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>) to add rows, <kbd>Shift</kbd> to select a range, or use the checkboxes. A bar above the rows then offers:

- **To Do**, **Done** and **Cancelled** for the selected tasks;
- **Move to…** another calendar;
- **Delete**, after asking.

For a repeating entry these change the selected occurrence only, except under **All entries**, where the row is the whole series. To change a series from any other span, open its editor.

<kbd>Escape</kbd> clears the selection and <kbd>Delete</kbd> deletes it.

## New entries

**Create** in the page header, or <kbd>C</kbd>, starts an entry for today and opens it as a row of the table.
